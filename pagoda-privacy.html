Privacy Policy

Pagoda collects no data.

The app makes no network connections. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party software of any kind inside it. Your game saves and statistics are stored only on your own device and are never transmitted anywhere.

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to sell, share, lose, or delete. If you remove the app, everything it ever knew about you goes with it.

If you have any questions about this policy, write to deanconnorl@icloud.com.

Effective September 1, 2026. If this policy ever changes, the change will be posted on this page before it takes effect.
