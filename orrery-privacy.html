PRIVACY POLICY

Orrery collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party code, and it makes no network connections of any kind.

Your progress — runs played, boards cleared, best score — is stored on your own device and never leaves it. Deleting the app deletes all of it.
