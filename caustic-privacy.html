CAUSTIC

Privacy policy · 19 September 2026

The short version

Caustic collects nothing, stores nothing about you, and sends nothing anywhere. It has no analytics, no advertising, no third-party code and no account, and it makes no network connection of its own. The exceptions are Apple's own services, both optional: Game Center, if you are signed in to it, and iCloud, which keeps your progress in step across your own devices.

Permissions

Caustic asks for no camera, microphone, location, contacts or notification access. The only input it ever receives is your finger on the screen.

There is one optional exception. In Free Play you can share a picture of the light you have made. If you choose Save Image from the share sheet, iOS asks whether Caustic may add photos to your library. That permission only lets the app add the picture you asked it to save — it cannot see, read or upload anything already in your photos — and you can decline it or turn it off at any time in Settings.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center on your device, Caustic can post your best Endless score, your time on each day's puzzle, how far you got in the day's run and how quickly you raced a chapter to its leaderboards, and mark achievements. The leaderboards show the name you use in Game Center, as all Game Center leaderboards do. Game Center is Apple's service and is covered by Apple's privacy policy; Caustic sends nothing to anyone else, and does not see or keep anything about you from it. The game never asks you to sign in, and works exactly the same without Game Center.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, the same small record described below - which levels you have finished, your medals and times - is also kept in your own iCloud account, so it follows you to your other devices. It is stored by Apple under your Apple Account; the developer cannot see it and never receives it. You can turn it off for Caustic in the iCloud section of Settings.

What is stored on your device

Which levels and daily puzzles you have finished and how, your best Endless score, daily times and chapter times, and your settings — so the game opens where you left off. Caustic also keeps a note of how you solved each level, as a short list of where your finger went, so that you can watch your own solve back or send it to someone. That is a handful of numbers, dates and coordinates. It stays on the device, and in your own iCloud if you use it, and it is removed from the device when you delete the app.

Solve codes

If you choose to share a solve, Caustic puts a code on the clipboard along with the picture, and it is yours to send or not. The code describes finger movements on a numbered level and nothing else: no name, no device and nothing that identifies anybody. A code someone sends you is only read when you tap to watch it.

The home screen widget

The widget shows today's puzzle and your streak. It reads those two things from the same record on your device and sends nothing anywhere.

Children

Caustic is rated 4+ and is safe for any age. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy ever changes it will be because the app gained a feature that needed it, and the change will be described here with a new date.
