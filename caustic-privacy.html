CAUSTIC

Privacy policy · 11 September 2026

The short version

Caustic collects nothing, stores nothing about you, and sends nothing anywhere. It has no analytics, no advertising, no third-party code, no account, and no network connection of any kind.

Permissions

None. Caustic asks for no permissions at all — no camera, no microphone, no location, no contacts, no notifications. The only input it ever receives is your finger on the screen.

What is stored on your device

Which levels you have finished, so the game opens where you left off. That is a list of numbers, it stays on the device, and it is removed when you delete the app.

Children

Caustic is rated 4+ and is safe for any age. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy ever changes it will be because the app gained a feature that needed it, and the change will be described here with a new date.
